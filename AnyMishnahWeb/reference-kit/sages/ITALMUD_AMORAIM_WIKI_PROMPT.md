## How to add what you find
Only in `kit_overrides.json`: `add_links` = `[slug, relation, slug]` with relation one of `teachers students colleagues
father children spouse in_laws sons_in_law siblings grandparents grandchildren`; `drop_links` the same shape. Never edit
`sages.json` by hand. Then `venv/bin/python sages_kit.py`, rebuild iTalmud, and tell the author to rebuild
Illustrated Mishna too (it reads the same kit). The family tree and the relationships box show new links automatically.

## Rules
- Facts only from Sefaria and Wikipedia, never Wikipedia's wording (CC BY-SA). If the two Wikipedias disagree (Hanina
  b. Gamliel: Hebrew says Gamliel II's son, English says not proved), leave it out and report it.
- Check each added link by opening the sage's family view (the Family pill in the relationships box).
- Do not apply a relation you are guessing from a name alone ("b. X" does not always mean son of the sage X).
