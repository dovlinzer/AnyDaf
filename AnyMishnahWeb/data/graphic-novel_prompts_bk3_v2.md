# Bava Kamma 3, graphic novel: first-try prompts v2 (men and women mixed on purpose) (2026-10-10)
Purpose: test whether the lessons from BK 5 / BM 1 give good pictures on the first try. Written from the mishnah text and R. Kulp's commentary only. Scenes: 3:1 to 3:7 and 3:11 (3:8, 3:9, 3:10 are about amounts and comparisons, left for a decision).
How to use: paste the block under PASTE into a new Gemini chat. Where it says 'same chat, edit', paste it into the chat that made the previous picture. 'Review' lists what to check before accepting; 'Overlay' says what we add in code.
CHANGE from v1: gender is now chosen on purpose in each scene (about half the main figures are women, in varied roles: pot-sellers, woodcutter, pourer, owners). Prompts that changed: 3:1a, 3:1b, 3:2a, 3:2b, 3:3, 3:4, 3:5 (all four), 3:6, 3:7, 3:11a, 3:11b; none is unchanged except the edit lines that never named a person.
Lessons applied: style block names no objects and carries a palette sentence; no standing negatives; the scene says what IS there (cracked into three pieces, hopping on one foot) not what is not; counts stated; every person has their own clothes; sets that change one fact are edits in the same chat; any left/right order is stated against a fixed direction of travel; no picture carries a quantity.
SOURCE NOTE: 3:5 clause 1 (side by side): R. Kulp's explanation says the beam owner is liable, which contradicts the mishnah text; the author checked and Kehati confirms the mishnah: he is EXEMPT. R. Kulp was wrong here. Kehati outranks R. Kulp wherever they differ.
