# Clay sages: colour versions (gold, blue, green) of each figure (2026-10-29)

Why: in the sages box each sage keeps his own picture when he is clicked and moves to the middle (gold), the teachers' row (blue) or the students' row (green). Today each figure exists in one colour only. These prompts make the other two colours, from the pictures you already approved, so a sage's clothing can match his role. 7 figures x 3 colours = 21; 7 exist, so 14 to make (15 with the optional teacher 2 redo below).

How to use: for each block, start a NEW Gemini chat, attach the picture named in the heading, and paste the PASTE text. Save each result as <figure>-<colour>.png (for example `main-blue.png`, `student-2-gold.png`) and send them to me; I cut out the backgrounds and wire them in. The reference pictures are saved in data/clay_sages/originals/.

Colour words (use the same ones every time, so the set matches): GOLD = warm golden-yellow like the main sage's tunic; BLUE = muted slate or steel blue like the teachers' robes; GREEN = leaf green like the students' tunics. Cloaks and head coverings keep a wool look: sandy for gold, grey-blue for blue, olive or sea-green for green. Skin, hair and beards never change.

Design choices, not from any source: all the colours.


Review: only the clothing colour changed; the face, hands, beard and pose are identical to the reference; plain white backdrop; one figure.

## teacher-2 (the bald teacher with the open hands, data/clay_sages/originals/teacher_270.jpg): a NOTE first

In the picture you approved, his face and hands are tinted blue-grey, like his tunic (they are not a natural skin tone). With a gold or green robe that would look odd. So these three prompts give him a natural skin tone on his face, ears and hands; that is the one deliberate change to a figure. If you would rather keep him blue-skinned, skip the skin sentence in each prompt; and for a matching set, make his blue version again too (the third prompt).
