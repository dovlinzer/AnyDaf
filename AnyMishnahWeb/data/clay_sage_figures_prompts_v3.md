What these are: generic portrait figures for the relationship box on the Mishnah pages (the sage in the middle, his teachers above, his students below). They are NOT pictures of a mishnah case, so there is no mishnah to read first; they are one look per ROLE, reused for every sage. (If we later want a distinct face for a famous sage, e.g. Rabbi Akiva, that is a separate prompt, and the Kehati/source-first rule applies to anything it shows.)

Three roles, three looks (author's request: a different colour, tint and look for main sage, teachers and students):
- MAIN SAGE: warm gold and cream, the largest and most vivid, with a plain wool cloak; kind, welcoming.
- TEACHERS: cool blue and slate, older, long white beard, softer and slightly muted; a hand raised as if explaining.
- STUDENTS (also sages, only younger than their teachers): fresh green and teal, middle-aged (about 40-50), short neat beard, softer and slightly muted; a plain wool tunic and cloak like the teachers.
Design choices, not from any source (list for review): the ages, the beard styles, the colours of the clothing, the scroll in a student's hand. Clothing follows the STYLE_BIBLE rule for the setting: plain wool tunics and cloaks, sandals; nothing tailored or modern.

Make 1 main-sage picture, 3 teacher pictures and 3 student pictures (the box picks among the variants so the same face does not repeat). Each variant has one distinguishing feature, stated in the prompt.

Technical: square, half-length (head to the waist), one figure, centred, on a plain flat pure-white backdrop with nothing else, so the background can be cut out and the figure placed on the page. Paste each PASTE block into a NEW Gemini chat. Send me the images; I remove the backgrounds and wire them in.
