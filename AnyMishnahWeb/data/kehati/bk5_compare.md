# BK 5 against Kehati (read 2026-10-12, PDF pp.46-61 plus 5:2 close-up). Applied to the site except where marked VERIFY (kept out or flagged inside the note).
- 5:1 done earlier (Sumchus; halakha = Sages).
- 5:2: Kulp's "implied" (visitor exempt for injuries to the householder's cattle, with permission) is stated outright by Kehati. The mishnah's "with permission, householder pays" rests on his having accepted to guard; the halakha follows Rabbi (5:3): only if he accepted to guard. Added to 5:2/5:3 halakha.
- 5:3: Rabbi = halakha. The pit and the ransom case: Kehati's reading (an ox mu'ad to fall on people) not applied (VERIFY: p.63-64).
- 5:4: Gemara limits R. Shimon b. Gamliel to a first birth; the freed bondwoman/convert explained (husband died with no heirs, payment ownerless). Marked VERIFY.
- 5:5: SITE LACKED: shallow pit that only injures = FULL damages (nezek shalem). Added. Opening decides domain, not bottom (Tosefta). The Kulp "exception for an intruder" is Kulp's, not in Kehati: unchanged, tagged Kulp.
- 5:6: "backward" = fell outside behind the pit (gerama); clever ox by day exempt, at night liable; trappings named (yoke, plough, saddle). Kehati: cover must stand ordinary wind.
- 5:7: nothing contradicts; added halakha for kilayim (Rambam).
- Not read: 6:1 onward (pp.62+ in the PDF numbering after 5:7; p.72 next).
