# Older-audience style test (2026-10-09)

Four styles x two scenes = 8 images. Run each in a NEW chat in Gemini, once, unedited. Then tell me: which style, what is off. The scene text is the same as the clay version that worked, so differences are the style's.
STYLE WORDS ARE PLAIN LOWERCASE, no role labels. Dead animals: on the side, eyes closed, still (no X eyes, no legs in the air: that was the children's convention).

## A. Graphic novel, ink and flat color / Scene 1: BK 5:1 (the cow, calf and ox)

Illustration for a Mishnah study site for teenagers and adults, in the style of a mature graphic novel: confident black ink outlines of varying weight, a muted limited palette of earth tones with one accent color, simple cell shading, dramatic framing, expressive but realistic faces, 3:2 wide format. No letters or words anywhere in the image, and no numerals. Every figure has a full face with eyes, nose and mouth. Land of Israel, 1st-century setting, simple tunics and cloaks, scrolls and never bound books. The sky or backdrop fills the whole background; no white background. Draw ONLY the people, animals and objects named in this prompt, and exactly the number named of each. Do NOT draw any badge, scale, coins, money or symbol of ownership, payment or splitting; those are added later. A dead animal lies on its side with its eyes closed, quite still (no cartoon marks). The picture has exactly two parts: a main scene in the lower part, and exactly two framed rounded boxes floating above it (no more, no fewer), with no tails, not thought bubbles, no icon in their corners. A farm yard. A gored cow lies on her side, eyes closed, quite still, and a dead newborn calf lies on its side beside her, eyes closed, quite still. An ox stands a little way off, head lowered, horns pointing toward the cow, steam coming from its nostrils. A farmer in a simple tunic scratches his head, puzzled. Above the scene, two boxes side by side that look clearly different, each showing an ox and a cow at normal size and proportion, the ox charging at the cow. Left box: the cow with a newborn calf already standing beside her. Right box: a visibly pregnant cow with a very round belly, and no calf. A single question mark, drawn in the same style as the rest of the picture, floats in the gap between the two boxes, outside both of them.
