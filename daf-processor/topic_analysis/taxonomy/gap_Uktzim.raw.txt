Looking at Mishnah Uktzim, which deals with the laws of handles (yadot), stems (uktzim/itztzuvanim), and connectors (beit hashelesha) as they relate to food's susceptibility to tumah and the transmission of tumah - I'll identify genuine gaps in the taxonomy.

Uktzim covers: which parts of produce (stalks, roots, shells, pits, husks) count as "handles" (yadot) to transmit tumah or become susceptible to it, which parts are considered part of the food itself, and which are considered separate. This is a specialized area of tumat ochlin law.
